# App Review notes for Strata

Paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes. Short, factual,
no defensiveness: what it is, how to test it.

---

Strata is an interactive map of the cosmos and recorded history, rendered
natively with Skia and fully offline: all content is bundled, no account, no
login, and no network access needed. It also includes a home-screen widget
showing a fact of the day from the bundled content.

Quick tour for review:
1. Launch, then tap "Start exploring".
2. Pinch out to see the whole scale; pinch in to dive into the Solar System.
3. Tap any dot to inspect it; tap a second dot to measure the gap between them.
4. Tap "Strata · Cosmos" in the header to switch to the History map.
5. Drag the bottom track to travel across the whole map in one move.
6. Optional: add the Strata widget from the home screen for the daily fact.

The app can be fully reviewed in Airplane Mode. This version contains no
in-app purchases.
